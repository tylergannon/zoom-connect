Overview
Zoom Connect — scope & MVP map
A web application with a Go service behind it (skgo). Its entire purpose is to live as a Zoom bot for a small company: listen to calls, keep transcripts, and serve them live and afterward to the company's people and their agents.
MVP line
Minimally listen to Zoom calls, and have another connected app or two listening in live — or downloading the finished transcript afterward.

Access model
The rule every screen and every API route enforces.
Who gets in
Google sign-in with a verified email at
ALLOWED_DOMAIN
(env var). Everyone else is refused.
What they see
Any call they were in, plus calls shared with them by someone who was in it.
Agents
An API key acts as its owner: same calls, live (SSE) and finished (download).
