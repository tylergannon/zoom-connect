Design notes
Purpose
Let a participant hand a call to a coworker who wasn't there. The link is the call's own URL; the share record is what opens it.
Behavior
Combobox searches people who have signed in; typing a full
@acme.co
address works for someone who hasn't signed in yet — the share waits for their first sign-in.
Primary action adds the person
and
copies the link, since the next step is always "paste it to them".
Participants can't be removed. A share can be removed by whoever granted it.
Grantees see this dialog read-only (no Add) — see open question Q2.
