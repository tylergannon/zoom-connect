Design notes
Purpose
Watch a call as it happens. The URL exists from the moment the call connects, so it can be pasted into chat (or a Slack DM, later) right away.
Behavior
Consecutive lines from one speaker group into a turn. Partial (non-final) lines show grey + caret and are replaced when final.
Scrolling up pauses follow; "Jump to live" returns. The pill shows Following / Paused / Reconnecting.
Joins, leaves, and stream interrupts appear as dividers. Chat lines are interleaved and also listed in the Chat tab.
"Listening now" shows the key name + prefix of each subscribed agent — visibility into who's tapping the call.
When the call ends the page swaps to the finished layout in place (no reload).
Scope
MVP. Claims U4, U7.

(U12). Guests and dial-ins are shown but never get access.
