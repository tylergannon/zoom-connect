Design notes
Purpose
One place to set up and keep an eye on the Zoom side, so nobody needs shell access to rotate a secret or find the webhook URL.
Behavior
Checklist ticks itself when the server has evidence (validation answered, signature verified, scopes seen); the account-setting step can't be verified and stays manual.
Secrets are write-only: after save, only the last 4 characters show. Env vars win and render read-only.
Rejected webhooks (bad signature, stale timestamp) surface in the log — the most common setup failure.
Non-admins never see the Admin sidebar group; the route 404s for them.
Scope
MVP. Claim U9.
Audio and shared-media capture are Post-MVP.
