Design notes
Purpose
Put the call link where people already are, at the two moments it's useful: start and finish.
Behavior
Only org members of the call (matched by email) get DMs; guests never do.
Late joiners get the start DM when they join; nobody gets it twice per call.
If the call was interrupted, the finish DM says “ended unexpectedly — partial transcript”.
Buttons are plain links to the call URL; sign-in still applies.

Post-MVP.
Claims U10, U11 (and the share DM from T22).
